Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026

What we use

This site does not set its own tracking cookies. It may use localStorage in your browser to remember your preferences (e.g. selected map layers, chosen date), which stays on your device and is never sent to a server.

Third parties

Map tile providers and any embedded analytics may set their own cookies, governed by their own policies — this site does not control those.

Managing cookies

You can clear cookies and local storage at any time through your browser's settings; doing so simply resets your saved preferences on this site.
