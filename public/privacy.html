Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

What we collect

This site displays publicly available geographic and event data on a map. Where a form is offered (e.g. to report or suggest a location), only the information you choose to submit is sent, via the channel stated on that form (email, WhatsApp, or Telegram) — nothing is stored on our servers, because this site has none: it is a static, client-only application.

Analytics

This site may use privacy-respecting, aggregate analytics to understand overall usage. No personally identifying data is collected or sold.

Third-party map tiles

Map tiles are loaded from third-party providers (e.g. OpenStreetMap, Esri). Loading a tile sends your IP address to that provider, subject to their own privacy policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact channel listed on the site, if any.
